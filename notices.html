印尼・沙馬林達（Samarinda, Indonesia）

🌤️ 10月天氣與穿著建議
October Weather & What to Wear

  • 沙馬林達緊鄰赤道，屬熱帶雨林氣候，全年氣溫偏高
    Samarinda sits near the equator with a tropical rainforest climate — hot year-round
  • 白天最高氣溫約 32°C～34°C，夜間低溫約 22°C～24°C，體感悶熱
    Daytime highs around 32–34°C, night lows 22–24°C, feels humid and hot
  • 10月為換季過渡期，降雨天數增加（平均單月約 11 天），常有午後強降雨或雷陣雨
    October is a transition month with more rain days (~11/month); expect sudden afternoon downpours or thunderstorms
  • 穿著建議：輕薄、透氣、吸汗的棉質或排汗衣物
    Wear: light, breathable, sweat-wicking cotton or athletic fabric
  • 室內冷氣通常很強，建議隨身攜帶薄外套
    Indoor air-con is often very strong — bring a light jacket
  • 必備：摺疊傘或輕便雨衣、墨鏡、防曬用品
    Bring: a compact umbrella or rain poncho, sunglasses, sunscreen
