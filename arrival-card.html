⚠️ 注意事項
Important Notes

  • 取得印尼入境卡 QR Code：完成填寫之後會出現一組 QR Code，請務必先截圖存在手機，也可以印出紙本放在隨身行李，入境印尼時要提供給海關掃描。
    Get your Arrival Card QR Code: After completing the form, a QR code will be issued. Be sure to screenshot and save it on your phone, or print a paper copy to carry with you. You'll need to show it for scanning at Indonesian immigration.
  • 如果未申請，影響入關後果自負。
    If you do not complete this form, you are responsible for any consequences at immigration.
