🔋 行動電源與鋰電池登機規定
Power Bank & Lithium Battery Rules

  • 所有行動電源與備用鋰電池，一律禁止託運，必須放在隨身行李帶上機
    All power banks & spare lithium batteries must be carried in cabin baggage — never in checked baggage
  • 每人最多攜帶 2 個 行動電源
    Max 2 power banks per passenger
  • 100Wh 以下（約 27,000mAh 以下）：可免申報隨身攜帶
    Under 100Wh (~27,000mAh): allowed in carry-on, no approval needed
  • 101Wh～160Wh：需經航空公司事先核准，限量攜帶
    101–160Wh: requires prior airline approval, limited quantity
  • 超過 160Wh：嚴格禁止攜帶上機
    Over 160Wh: strictly prohibited on board
  • 備用電池請單獨包裝，避免短路
    Spare batteries must be individually packaged to prevent short-circuiting
